# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We welcome contributors and participants regardless of disability, age, body size, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation. Participation should be respectful, safe and accessible.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate of different experiences and access needs. Use welcoming language, give constructive feedback, accept corrections and focus on the shared work. Respect privacy and consent, including when discussing a person's disability or assistive technology. Provide accessible alternatives for contribution activities where possible.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and repeated disruptive conduct are unacceptable. Do not dismiss a person's access needs or demand personal medical information.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, reviews and project events, and when someone represents the project publicly. Maintainers are responsible for fair enforcement and may edit or remove harmful contributions, issue warnings, restrict participation or ban participants for severe or repeated misconduct.

Report incidents privately through the maintainer's published contact when available. **Maintainer action needed: publish a private conduct-reporting contact.** No contact address is invented here. Avoid posting sensitive incident details publicly; if necessary, request a private contact without identifying the affected person.

Maintainers should protect reporter privacy, consider evidence and context, communicate decisions respectfully, and offer a review of enforcement decisions through a different maintainer where available. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is unacceptable.
